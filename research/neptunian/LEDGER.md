# Neptunian Ledger

Neptunian is an executable investigation, not an ontology. This ledger preserves the experimental through-line cheaply enough that interruptions do not become architecture.

## Crossing 001

The working crossing is:

**Builder₀ → Observer₀ → Painter₀ → Builder₁**

The first crossing includes bootstrap work that later crossings should not require. Builder₀ has already run. Observer₀ is currently being constructed as a reusable instrument. The Observer pass has **not** run yet. Painter₀ must not be designed until a cold Observer has produced evidence through the actual sensing aperture.

### Builder₀

Builder₀ created two ordinary independent forms inside one Betwixtable: a small bridge and an illuminated beacon on a quiet glass support. They were authored as normal Three.js geometry using Workshop materials and deliberately carry no Observer-facing semantic identity.

The construction is already complete. Knowledge held by the warm Builder must not be smuggled into the later Observer pass.

### Observer₀ bootstrap

The bounded Witness/Observer currently exposes anonymous geometric consequence rather than scene identity. Sensing returns distance, hit point, and surface normal. Names, object IDs, hierarchy, materials, source coordinates, semantic components, and Witness-owned geometry are withheld.

The Observer has a visible but self-blind body, finite deliberate-action budget, pose/transcript support, and belongs to the same rigid Crossing 001 Betwixtable frame as the specimen.

Observer locomotion is being changed from arbitrary translation into environmental idle behavior. Build `70a3990c` introduces a deterministic slowly precessing spherical orbit around the Betwixtable experiment. While idle, the Observer continuously faces the experiment center and leaves a faint persistent trajectory that is visible to humans but excluded from its own sensing. Over time the orbit should weave a spherical shell of viewpoints.

The intended separation is:

- **idle locomotion is free, deterministic, and center-facing;**
- **deliberate attention is bounded and recorded;**
- deliberate observation will be able to pause the idle orbit, redirect gaze, sense, then release back into center-facing orbit.

At `70a3990c`, the motion/trace is implemented for visual evaluation. The older `look` / `move` calls still exist temporarily; idle reasserts its pose on the next frame. Do not mistake that transitional API for the final Observer contract.

Once the motion is accepted, finish intentional pause/aim/sense semantics here. Then stop warm construction.

The next actual experimental act is a **cold-window Observer pass**: a fresh Clara operates the real Observer sensor suite against Crossing 001 without being told what Builder₀ made. Its perceptual evidence/account becomes the only legitimate basis for deciding what Painter₀ needs to be.

## Interruptions absorbed during Observer construction

Observer construction was interrupted by productive Betwixt pressure rather than abandoned.

### Betwixtable interaction

Foreground/focus behavior was tightened so Betwixtables use coherent presentation bounds, transform-correct foreground placement, modal foreground taps, and focused-object first refusal. These changes improved the spatial substrate Crossing 001 itself depends on; they are not new Neptunian semantics.

### Chem Lab presentation

Chem Lab's molecule information card was moved clear of the spell containers. The temporary element/color glance board was cleaned up as presentation residue rather than chemistry authority.

During a family demonstration, John's mother asked to add **Platinum**. Pt joined H/C/O/N on the live Chem Lab board. Her small plain-language contribution became a useful real demonstration of the system's intended conversational construction loop.

Immediately afterward the board was made data-driven: its width and card placement derive from the element specification list, so adding another element no longer requires manually rebuilding the container. This remains presentation machinery, not chemical ontology.

### Publication membrane hardening

The Platinum/dynamic-board crossing exposed a deployment truth: Interstice main containing a new build is not equivalent to the public Pages membrane serving it.

The permanent publication contract was hardened. "Live" now means the public Betwixt Pages membrane has been fetched and verified to serve the exact expected Repository provenance/build. Repository success, Home pinning, Interstice source publication, or workflow completion alone are intermediate states.

Normal coordination uses the exact eight-character HUD build identity. Do not claim a build is live until the public membrane proves it.

### Cold handoff

The broader Home/Repository environment has already reached the useful cold-handoff threshold. Do not confuse that with the experiment now pending.

What remains here is narrower and more interesting: after Observer₀ construction is complete, deliberately open a fresh context and let a cold model inhabit the Observer's actual bounded sensor suite. No warm recap of the specimen should cross that boundary.

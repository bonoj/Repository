# Vehicle Box

Free to a good home.

These are the 25 authored low-poly vehicle bodies produced during Crucible's temporary Extruder Yard design pass. They share the orbital station's material language and were evaluated directly under Crucible lighting rather than as concept renders.

The yard itself is preserved in `extruder-yard.js` as dormant design apparatus. It is not imported by the live runtime.

Candidate 25, the corner farthest from the discarded prototype in the yard arrangement, was selected as the active extruder body. The original production extruder geometry was discarded.

Nothing here is canonical world vocabulary merely because it exists. Reuse a body when a future system earns it.

Hero instinct

Ignus and Drakkan remain conspicuous high-end picks; Zefira is a superb mobile control partner. Pair jobs rather than two copies of “damage.”

Spell shell

Reinforcements + Rain of Fire cover time and deletion. The third slot should be displacement, control, summon, or utility according to what your loadout lacks.

All fifteen. Each card answers: what job buys this pad, where does it want to live, and what makes it worth upgrading?

Heroes are movable infrastructure. Their value is not just DPS—it is how cheaply they repair the map while your towers keep doing their jobs.

Three slots, three distinct emergencies. A spell is strongest when it prevents you from buying permanent infrastructure for a temporary problem.

Placement is an economy multiplier. A tower covering twice as much useful path is effectively cheaper.

1 · Establish

Minimum jobs that stop leaks: coverage + real stall + required damage type.

2 · Deepen

Upgrade towers already exploiting the choke before opening another mediocre position.

3 · Specialize

Then buy armor shred, true damage, support, economy, anti-flyer, or a second pocket.

Don't panic-carpet

First ask whether moving a hero/rally or spending one cooldown fixes the failure for free.

Diagnostic: many occupied pads + nothing feels strong = stop expanding. One strong pocket + one specific enemy escapes = buy the counter, not generic DPS.

Upgrade the family you repeatedly field. A full path in a tower-family tree costs 14 points; specialization pays when your five-tower loadout actually uses it.

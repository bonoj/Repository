# The Summerlands map — campaign source document

*2026-10-12 · Hermeticon Wanderer · historical source and future worldbuilding input*

## Provenance
The source is a hand-drawn, colored campaign map made by the human DM for a week-long Renaissance Adventures quest in Boulder, Colorado, involving his daughters Lex and Oli and four other children. It was **drawn before the week began**, so the children could sense the breadth of the world and anticipate adventures. Children or parents later requested copies. This document records the source, not a reconstruction of the campaign's events.

**Important geographic correction:** **The Summerlands is only the yellow area on the map**, **not the name of the entire depicted world**. Do not title or generate the whole map as “The Summerlands.” Preserve the distinction between the yellow region and the surrounding locations.

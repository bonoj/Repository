# The Summerlands

*World document · Hermeticon Wanderer · 2026-10-12*

## Origin

The Summerlands is a hand-drawn campaign world created by the human collaborator while serving as a quest leader at Renaissance Adventures in Boulder. It was one of two worlds he led Lex, Oli, and four other children through during separate weeks.

**This map was drawn before the adventure week began.** Its purpose was to let the children immediately sense a wider world and anticipate adventures they might have. The companion campaign, *Empire of the Seven Stars*, was mapped progressively from a blank page as the children traveled.

In both campaigns, children or parents requested copies of the maps by email or text. The map is therefore both a prepared invitation and an artifact of a shared experience. Do not recast it as AI-generated or claim that the locations below were procedurally authored.

## Cartographic transcription

Title: **The Summerlands**.

Named regions and landmarks, transcribed from the original image (some lettering is handwritten and warrants checking against the image before treating as canonical):

- **The Winter King's Realm** — icy mountains in the far northwest.
- **To Ice Lake** — arrow pointing north.
- **The Gradient** — broad curved directional arrow near the northwest.
- **The Everlasting Fields** — yellow rolling country in the west.
- **The Quills** — dark spired belt separating the western fields from the interior.
- **Upton** — settlement near the northern coast.
- **Redwall** — long pink-red wall/road along the northern boundary.
- **The Rift** — central-northern basin or fractured country.
- **The Seil** — coastal feature northeast of the Rift; lettering uncertain.
- **The House of Darkness** — red/pink formation in the central interior.
- **The Blind Lakes** — chain of pink pools south of the House of Darkness.
- **The Whisperwood** — woodland between the Rift and the eastern forests; lettering slightly uncertain.
- **The Brambles** — dense green eastern woodland.
- **The Ocean of Tears** — eastern open sea.
- **The Violet Coast** — eastern shoreline.
- **The Isle of Fire** — northeastern island with **Kindle Tower**.
- **Jade Peak** — western offshore highlands.
- **The Mists** — western archipelago.
- **Downton** — southwestern settlement or structure.
- **Silverpass and the Geysers** — southern mountain pass and geothermal features.
- **Cinderash and the Fell Crater** — southeastern volcanic region; first word uncertain.
- **To the Empire of the Seven Stars** — arrow pointing east, linking this world to the second campaign.
